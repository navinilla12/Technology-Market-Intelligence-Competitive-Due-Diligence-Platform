# Technology Market Intelligence & Competitive Due Diligence Platform

## Overview

This project simulates a real-world market intelligence and technology due diligence platform used by consulting firms, venture capital organizations, and corporate strategy teams.

The system analyzes startup funding data to identify market trends, evaluate competitive positioning, benchmark companies, and generate strategic business insights.

---

## Business Problem

Organizations need a systematic approach to:

- Evaluate emerging technology markets
- Assess startup ecosystems
- Identify investment opportunities
- Benchmark competitors
- Generate market intelligence reports

Manual research is time-consuming and difficult to scale.

---

## Solution

This platform automates market research and competitive intelligence analysis through:

- Funding trend analysis
- Investor activity analysis
- Sector performance benchmarking
- Company due diligence scoring
- Executive reporting
